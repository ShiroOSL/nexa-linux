# Training Data & Voice Collection

Nexa can optionally save short "Hey Nexa" wake word clips on your device (see **Settings > Privacy**). This file explains exactly what that data is used for.

## What is collected

- Short audio clips of you saying "Hey Nexa" (the wake word), saved locally on your own computer.
- Nothing is collected or uploaded automatically. Clips stay on your device until you choose to export and send them.

## What it is used for

- Only to improve the accuracy of the "Hey Nexa" wake word model.
- Nothing else. It is not used for any other feature, profiling, or analytics.

## What happens if you choose to send it to me

- You export the clips yourself (Settings > Privacy > Export Training Data) and email them to me at hello.nexa.assistant@gmail.com, entirely optional.
- I use the clips only to train and improve the wake word model.
- Once training is done, I delete the clips. I do not keep a permanent copy.

## What I do NOT do

- I do not sell your voice data.
- I do not share it with third parties.
- I do not use it for anything other than wake word training.
- I do not keep it after training is complete.

## Your control

- Exporting and sending clips is entirely optional and off by default and stays local unless you choose to share it.
- You can clear saved clips at any time from Settings > Privacy > Clear.
